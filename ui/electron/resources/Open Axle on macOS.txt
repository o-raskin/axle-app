Open Axle on macOS

1. Drag Axle to Applications, then open Axle from Applications.
2. If macOS says Apple could not verify Axle, choose Done.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to Security.
4. Choose Open Anyway for Axle, then confirm Open. Authenticate if asked.

If Open Anyway is missing, try opening Axle again. The option appears for
about an hour after an attempted launch. Future launches use the same
approval; a new version may require approval again.

Axle is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. Keeping these
builds free of Apple Developer credentials means the first-launch approval
cannot be removed. Approve only a release you trust; compare its download
checksum with SHA256SUMS on the release page.

Apple's instructions:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

If graphical approval is unavailable, this Terminal fallback removes
quarantine only from your trusted Axle copy in Applications, then opens it:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Axle.app
open /Applications/Axle.app
