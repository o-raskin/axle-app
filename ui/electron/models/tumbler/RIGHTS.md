## What this repository establishes

The author publication and local hashes are recorded in
[source/LICENSE-EVIDENCE.md](source/LICENSE-EVIDENCE.md) and
`source/provenance.json`. The assembly is treated as CC BY-NC 4.0 material;
its original bytes and the LDraw contributor notices are retained. Changes
are described in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and the generated part notices.
CAD/part paths in those records identify entries in `source/cad-source.zip`;
lossless packing retains their bytes and original headers rather than replacing
their licenses. Archive extraction restores those paths for inspection/editing.

and [contributor agreement](https://www.ldraw.org/article/349.html) describe the
library licenses; they do not prove a license for unidentified Studio meshes.

## What remains unresolved

The post's Creative Commons footer is evidence of the publisher's license
choice, not proof of ownership of everything inside its attachments. Some
custom files name different creators; others identify none. No separate
license or consent from those creators is recorded. No permission covering
the underlying LEGO/DC design, trademarks, or markings is recorded either.

The [CC BY-NC legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en)
licenses only rights the licensor can grant. Its noncommercial condition
depends on the use, not simply whether the app costs money. A public GitHub
repository makes material available to others; the same unresolved rights
still matter there. Apache-licensed source code does not make its bundled
CC BY-NC asset suitable for unrestricted commercial reuse.

Attribution, a disclaimer, a trademark symbol and noncommercial intent cannot
create missing permission. LEGO's [Fair Play guidance](https://www.lego.com/en-us/legal/notices-and-policies/fair-play)
expressly says a disclaimer cannot repair improper trademark use. These
documents reduce incorrect ownership/affiliation claims and preserve known
terms; they cannot guarantee protection from a complaint or lawsuit.

## Distribution practice

Keep [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), this record, source provenance, and generated
part notices with model distributions, including compact source bundles and
app packages. Preserve original license/author/history lines inside source
bundles and identify subsequent modifications. Do not claim that the whole
repository's assets are Apache-2.0 or commercially cleared.

For wider or commercial distribution, obtain the relevant missing permissions
or replace the affected geometry/design with independently cleared material.
The right to retain/use this asset under the available evidence should be
reviewed for the intended jurisdiction and use if release certainty is needed.
Rights-holder correction/removal requests should be routed to the repository
maintainer as described in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
